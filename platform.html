Better practice for every Cambodian student.

LionholdAI creates practice questions for the Khmer curriculum and grades students’ papers automatically, so useful feedback no longer has to wait.

What it does

Two hard problems. One platform.

Endless, relevant practice for students, and fast, fair marking for everyone.

01

Curriculum-aligned questions

Generates practice questions mapped to the Khmer curriculum by grade, subject and topic, so students practise what they are actually taught.

02

Automatic grading

Marks student papers against clear criteria and returns feedback explaining what was right, what was missed and how to improve.

03

Progress insight

Shows students, and the teachers who support them, which topics are secure and which need more practice.

See it in action

From paper to feedback in seconds.

Every marked paper shows a student exactly what to do next.

  • A score for the paper, question by question
  • A plain explanation of every mistake
  • Suggested practice on the topics that need it

Our principles

Built for Cambodia, from day one.

01

Curriculum first

Every question starts from what students are expected to learn, not from what is easiest for a machine to produce.

02

Made for everyday devices

LionholdAI is designed to work on the phones and computers students already use, so access does not depend on special equipment.

03

Teachers in the loop

The platform is designed to support teachers and never to replace their judgement.
