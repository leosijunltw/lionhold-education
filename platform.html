In development

AI learning platform

Practice, written and marked by AI.

We are building an AI platform for students that creates questions for the Khmer curriculum and grades their papers automatically, so useful feedback no longer has to wait.

What it does

Two hard problems. One platform.

Endless, relevant practice for students, and fast, fair marking for everyone.

01

Curriculum-aligned questions

Generates practice questions mapped to the Khmer curriculum by grade, subject and topic, so students practise what they are actually taught.

02

Automatic grading

Marks student papers against clear criteria and returns feedback explaining what was right, what was missed and how to improve.

03

Progress insight

Shows students, and the teachers who support them, which topics are secure and which need more practice.

How it works

From question to feedback in four steps.

01

Choose

Pick a subject, grade and topic from the Khmer curriculum.

02

Practise

Receive a fresh set of questions generated for that topic.

03

Submit

Hand in your answers and have the paper graded automatically.

04

Improve

Review feedback, see your progress and focus on what to practise next.

Our principles

Built for Cambodian classrooms.

01

Curriculum first

Every question starts from what students are expected to learn, not from what is easiest for a machine to produce.

02

Teachers in the loop

The platform is designed to support teachers and never to replace their judgement.

03

Clear, fair feedback

Grading should be consistent and explain itself, so students learn from every mark.
