School consulting

Practical advice for the people who run schools.

Education, operations and technology consulting for school leaders who want to improve how their school teaches, runs and uses technology.

Three lenses on one school

Every engagement starts with your priorities, not a template.

Choose one area or combine all three. We scope the work around what matters most to your school.

01

Education consulting

Reviewing curriculum delivery, assessment and teaching practice, and helping schools set priorities that improve student outcomes.

Questions we help with

  • How closely does our teaching follow the curriculum?
  • Are our assessments telling us what students really know?
  • Where should we invest in teacher development?

What you receive

  • A written review with clear findings
  • A prioritised action plan
  • Follow-up support to put it into practice
02

Operations consulting

Improving how a school is run, from enrolment and finance to staffing and day-to-day processes, so leaders can spend more time on teaching and learning.

Questions we help with

  • How can we simplify enrolment and admissions?
  • Does our budget match our priorities?
  • Are timetables and staffing working for teachers?

What you receive

  • A map of how your school runs today
  • Improvements ordered by impact
  • Documented processes your team can maintain
03

Technology consulting

Selecting, introducing and getting real value from digital tools, data and AI, including adopting our own AI learning platform.

Questions we help with

  • Which digital tools are actually worth paying for?
  • How can data help us support each student?
  • Is AI right for our school, and how do we introduce it responsibly?

What you receive

  • A review of your tools, with a shortlist
  • A rollout plan including staff training
  • The option to pilot our AI learning platform
