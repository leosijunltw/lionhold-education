University application support

Apply with clarity, in your own words.

Personal guidance for students applying to universities in Cambodia and abroad, from choosing where to apply to walking into the interview.

Your application journey

Four steps from shortlist to submission.

We walk alongside you at each stage, so nothing is left to the last minute.

  1. 1

    Explore

    Identify the universities and programmes that fit your goals, grades and budget, and find the scholarships worth applying for.

  2. 2

    Plan

    Work back from every deadline to build a realistic timeline, with a clear checklist of documents for each application.

  3. 3

    Write

    Shape your own story into personal statements and essays that sound like you and say what matters.

  4. 4

    Apply and interview

    Check forms, CVs and supporting documents before you submit, then practise admissions interviews with honest feedback.

How we help

Three kinds of support. Use one or all.

01

School and programme selection

Helping students identify universities and programmes that fit their goals, grades and budget, find relevant scholarships, and plan a realistic application timeline.

Includes

  • A shortlist matched to your goals, grades and budget
  • Scholarship and funding search
  • An application calendar with every deadline
02

Personal statements and essays

Working with students to shape their own story into clear, compelling statements and essays. The writing always remains the applicant’s own.

Includes

  • Brainstorming sessions to find your story
  • Feedback on every draft
  • A final read for clarity, tone and accuracy
03

Applications and interviews

Reviewing application forms, CVs and supporting documents, and preparing students for admissions interviews with practice and honest feedback.

Includes

  • Review of forms, CVs and supporting documents
  • Mock admissions interviews
  • Candid feedback after every practice round

Our promise

Honest guidance. Your own application.

What we do

  • Give guidance, feedback and editing support
  • Help you present your achievements clearly
  • Keep you organised against every deadline

What we don’t do

  • Write your essays for you
  • Guarantee admission, scholarships or visas
  • Submit anything you haven’t checked yourself

Applicants remain responsible for the accuracy and originality of everything they submit.
