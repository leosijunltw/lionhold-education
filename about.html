About Lionhold

Cambodian roots. International perspective.

Founded by three Cambodians who previously lived and worked in London, Lionhold brings together a shared understanding of finance, technology and international education.

Our story

From Cambridge to Cambodia.

Lionhold has its roots at the University of Cambridge, where two of its founders began building the company while completing their Master’s degrees.

Alongside their studies, the founders worked with Lionhold’s first clients—turning academic insight and professional experience into practical solutions for real commercial needs.

Lionhold Education brings that experience to students and schools. Based in Phnom Penh, it combines AI-driven learning tools, hands-on application support and consulting for schools. Its work is shaped by Cambodian ambition, international experience and a belief that better evidence leads to better decisions.
